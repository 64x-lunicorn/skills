Add `write-term`: when a word settles on one fixed meaning during an interview, a triage or an architecture review, the term is recorded in the `CONTEXT.md` at the project root as one line `**Term**: meaning.`, appended after the existing terms, once Daniel has confirmed the exact line. Words to avoid follow on the same line as `_Avoid_: x, y.` only when another word for the term actually came up, never guessed. A plain word, a term already present and a line Daniel has not confirmed record nothing, and a project without a root `CONTEXT.md` is pointed to `write-agent-docs`. The `CONTEXT.md` template of `write-agent-docs` describes the optional words to avoid.
